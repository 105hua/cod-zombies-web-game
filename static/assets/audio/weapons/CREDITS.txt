Mechanical sources are M1911A1 .45 ACP and Browning Belgian semi-automatic
12-gauge handling. Pistol magazine/slide textures are reused at a lower
playback rate for the rifle. The shotgun pump uses adapted bolt-open/close
textures, not a claimed recording of an actual Model 12 pump. Event timing
follows the game's visible magazine, shell-feed, slide and pump animations.

Adaptations for Dead Signal: isolated events; mono downmix; 48 kHz, 16-bit PCM;
55 Hz gunshot / 90 Hz mechanical high-pass filtering; peak normalization;
short boundary fades; gunshot tails trimmed to 0.72 s (pistol), 0.56 s (rifle)
and 0.92 s (shotgun). Runtime mixing varies shot playback rate by up to 0.3%
and gain by up to 4%, retaining the original recorded reports and reflections.
No synthetic gunshot bass sweep or added cinematic reverb is used.
