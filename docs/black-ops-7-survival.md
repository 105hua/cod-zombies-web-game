# Black Ops 7: named Survival returns

[Research index](./README.md) · [Game comparison](./game-comparison.md) · [BOII Survival](./classic-survival.md)

## Scope and evidence baseline

This is a focused case study of **Vandorn Farm Survival**, not a complete survey of Black Ops 7's seasonal content. The principal source is Activision's map guide dated **2025-09-30**. That guide discusses both beta conditions and the intended launch mode; its beta-only giveaways must not be treated as ordinary live-game rules. The November 2025 launch article separately documents the game's loadout and weapon-upgrade systems. [B7-1, B7-2]

## What Survival means here

Activision explicitly says Survival works largely like Standard round-based Zombies, except that it restricts players to **one location taken from a larger map**. Vandorn Farm is a location from Ashes of the Damned, with utility placement adjusted for the smaller experience. The Survival guide describes no quests or Easter eggs to pursue: the purpose is to survive the horde for as long as possible, solo or with a squad. [B7-1]

This is not simply a nickname for playing the full Ashes of the Damned map while ignoring its quest. It is a distinct, confined play space and mode configuration.

## What a typical farm run involves

The following sequence is **synthesis** of Activision's tactical advice, not a mandatory build order:

1. **Orient within the confined arena.** Learn the barn, ground-level routes, entrances, utility locations, and escape options. You cannot solve every bad position by traveling to another major region.
2. **Build resources while clearing rounds.** Seek opportunities to improve equipment without stopping in a dangerous place.
3. **Upgrade repeatedly.** The guide specifically calls out Pack-a-Punch, weapon rarity at the Arsenal, Perk-a-Colas, and replenishing supplies. Modern upgrades remain part of this stripped-down mode.
4. **Use vertical separation and traps.** Move between the barn's elevation and ground level to make pursuers travel farther. The Blade Trap is an additional survival tool.
5. **Repeat as rounds become harder.** If leaving one enemy alive creates time to purchase upgrades, do so quickly; that enemy is still a threat, not a paused game. [B7-1]

The separate launch article documents choosing a main firearm and melee weapon before a Zombies match, as well as Pack-a-Punch, rarity increases, and Ammo Mods. Thus the mode's classic-style arena framing should not be confused with a mandatory World at War-style pistol start. [B7-2]

## How it differs from BOII's named Survival

| Aspect             | BOII Green Run Survival                                                                                      | BO7 Vandorn Farm Survival                                                                  |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Spatial structure  | Small, standalone versions of Bus Depot, Farm, or Town.                                                      | One constrained location adapted from a larger round-based map.                            |
| Main activity      | Stay alive through rounds rather than follow TranZit's traversal/setup.                                      | Stay alive through rounds rather than follow Ashes of the Damned's main quest.             |
| Upgrade access     | Deliberately uneven: no perks or Pack-a-Punch at Bus Depot; perks but no Pack-a-Punch at Farm; both at Town. | The official guide explicitly recommends perks, Pack-a-Punch, and Arsenal rarity upgrades. |
| Combat preparation | Classic in-match acquisition and points economy.                                                             | Modern pre-match loadout and multiple in-match upgrade tracks.                             |
| Map knowledge      | Compact loops, bottlenecks, purchases, and hazards.                                                          | Compact loops plus barn elevation and the Blade Trap.                                      |

Sources: [B7-1, B7-2] and the map-specific references in [Classic Survival](./classic-survival.md). **BOII Farm and BO7 Vandorn Farm are not interchangeable rule sets.**

## What not to import from adjacent modes

The official guide names Standard, Directed, Survival, Cursed, and Dead Ops Arcade 4 as distinct experiences. It does not justify treating a main-quest walkthrough, Directed progression rule, or Cursed modifier as the default Survival rule. Likewise, complimentary GobbleGums described for the beta are explicitly limited to that beta and do not alter ordinary BO6/BO7 inventories. [B7-1]

The live BO7 Zombies landing page also lists additional Survival maps, including Mars and Ashwood. Their existence confirms that Vandorn Farm is not the full current catalog; it does not prove that every map has the same geometry, enemies, machine placement, or optional rules. This chapter intentionally avoids claiming a complete seasonal map list. [B7-3]

## Boundaries of this case study

The cited map guide does not supply a complete numerical ruleset for enemy populations, health scaling, revive losses, or extraction timing. No BO6 exfil schedule, damage table, or self-revive limit is silently reused here. These omissions limit exact simulation fidelity, not the central finding: **BO7's named Survival compresses modern round-based combat and upgrading into a confined, quest-free arena.**
