# Modern round-based Survival: Cold War and Black Ops 6

[Research index](./README.md) · [Cross-game comparison](./game-comparison.md)

## Scope and historical baseline

**Research cutoff/access: 2026-09-18.** This is representative round-based play, not exhaustive coverage or current balance. Cold War uses October 2020 launch explanations, a 2021 Die Maschine guide, and the identified Season Two skill expansion. BO6 uses August–November 2024 official explanations. Prices, spawn frequencies, damage tuning, and later additions should not be assumed unchanged.

**Cold War's Outbreak** advances through objective-driven regions; **Modern Warfare III's MWZ** is open-world PvE extraction. Neither structure should be substituted for Die Maschine, Liberty Falls, or Terminus rounds. [M9] [M10]

## Black Ops Cold War (2020)

### Opening: establish a route and upgrade access

**Die Maschine** combines an outdoor starting area, ruined building, and underground facility. Players enter with one customized Common/Loadout-rarity weapon and a Zombies-specific Field Upgrade. Improve its rarity or replace it through wall purchases and the Mystery Box. [M1] [M2]

Early Essence pays for doors and debris clearance on the way to power. Die Maschine then requires terminals, travel through the Dark Aether, and retrieval of a machine part to restore Pack-a-Punch. This is a map-specific setup sequence, not the universal requirement for all modern maps. [M2]

**Synthesis:** The opening balances buying enough killing power against funding power, escape space, and upgrades. A survival run does not require completing the story quest. [M1] [M2]

### Midgame: two economies and separate damage tracks

- **Essence** buys access, weapons, perks, and Pack-a-Punch. Enemy-dropped **Salvage** funds Arsenal improvements and crafting. Cold War has normal **green Salvage** and **blue High-Grade Salvage**; higher-end upgrades require the latter. [M2] [M4]
- **Rarity and Pack-a-Punch are independent, stacking upgrades.** The Arsenal raises rarity and improves armor; Pack-a-Punch has three weapon upgrade levels. Cold War also sells Ammo Mods at Pack-a-Punch, adding effects such as freezing, burning, or turning enemies. [M2]
- **Armor mitigates damage but has finite durability.** Cold War's three armor levels are upgraded through the Arsenal using Salvage. Dropped shards and Arsenal repairs replenish it; Carpenter fully restores it. This is not BO6's reserve-plate insertion loop. [M1] [M2]
- **Perks supplement health and handling.** Jugger-Nog increases maximum health; Quick Revive assists health recovery and teammate revives. Successive perk purchases become more expensive. Armor, perk skill tiers, and round-dependent enemy damage prevent universal hit-count claims. [M1] [M2]
- **Field Upgrades charge through kills**, rather than merely waiting. Examples include Aether Shroud for temporary concealment and Ring of Fire for damage amplification. Crafting tables convert resources into tacticals, lethals, and Support such as a Chopper Gunner or Self-Revive. [M1]

### Escalation, recovery, and endings

Die Maschine's Plaguehounds disrupt movement with gas; the **Megaton** splits into two enemies after its first health bar is depleted. Rounds become harder. **Synthesis:** Maintain damage, armor, ammunition, and maneuvering space. Training a horde around a route, turning to damage it, and returning to movement is one common pattern, not a mandatory or guaranteed-safe strategy. [M1] [M2]

Teammates or Self-Revive can rescue downed players, while perks can decay. Later skills change recovery—for example, upgraded Healing Aura preserves perks still on the decay meter. Do not assume these benefits for an unupgraded launch character. [M1] [M3]

**Exfil is optional, not a victory requirement.** The launch guide offers it after round 10 and every five rounds thereafter. A co-op majority must agree, then reach and clear the landing zone under a timer. Failure to clear it fails extraction. Players may instead push rounds until they cannot survive. [M1] [M2]

### Between matches: permanent skill tiers

Earned **Aetherium Crystals** permanently upgrade weapon classes, perks, Ammo Mods, and Field Upgrades. Launch skills had three tiers; Season Two added tiers IV/V using Refined/Flawless crystals. These account-level improvements differ from purchases made during one match. [M1] [M3]

## Black Ops 6 (2024)

### Opening and representative map differences

BO6 retains a chosen starting firearm, adding a dedicated melee weapon, Field Upgrade, tactical, lethal, and GobbleGum pack to its launch loadout. Wall/box weapons can use saved **Zombie Builds**. [M4] [M5]

**Liberty Falls** branches from a gas-station start toward the church's Pack-a-Punch. **Terminus** is a prison/research island with power objectives, a Bio Lab, boats, and outer islands. Neither Die Maschine's machine-part quest nor Terminus's power setup applies universally. Signature weapons differ: Liberty Falls has the **Thrustodyne Model 23**, Terminus the **Beamsmasher**. [M4] [M5] [M6]

### Midgame: familiar categories, different implementations

BO6's official systems guide explicitly replaces Cold War's two Salvage types with **one Salvage currency**, with player-specific drops. Essence still buys access, perks, ammunition, weapons, and three Pack-a-Punch levels. Salvage buys **rarity upgrades and Ammo Mods at the Arsenal**, plus equipment and Support at crafting tables. Thus, buying an Ammo Mod is no longer Cold War's Pack-a-Punch transaction. [M4]

**Armor capacity is an Essence-funded wall purchase:** level II/III vests support two/three worn plates. Separately looted or crafted plates replenish protection when used, unlike Cold War's automatic shard repair. Speed Cola accelerates replating and reloads. [M4] [M5]

Jugger-Nog raises maximum health; Quick Revive shortens regeneration delay and teammate revival time. Perk prices increase with purchases; downs cause time-dependent perk losses. Kill-charged Field Upgrades, distractions, and Support complement guns. The **Mutant Injection** temporarily transforms players into Manglers. October's launch specification limits **crafted Self-Revives to three per match**, not every possible revival. [M4] [M5]

### High rounds, modes, and endings

Later rounds bring faster zombies, larger hordes, and more armored/special threats. Terminus features Amalgams; Liberty Falls features Abominations, alongside Manglers and Vermin. **Synthesis:** Maintain both damage tracks, replate safely, control dangerous enemies, and retain a rescue option. No universal health formula or elite spawn schedule is asserted here. [M4] [M8]

**Standard** permits escalating survival and optional quest exploration. **Directed Mode**, introduced for the launch maps with Season One, marks main-quest objectives and gates progression, with a maximum round cap of 15. Capped waves repeat until objectives advance. Side quests, Trials, and **Rampage** are disabled. The Rampage Inducer separately accelerates zombie appearance/movement; it is optional, not another mode's name. Its duration is patch-sensitive. [M4] [M7] [M8]

Teammate revives, Self-Revive, and Healing Aura offer recovery; without a remaining recovery route, the run ends. **Optional exfil** deliberately ends a session: Liberty Falls first offers it on round 11, requiring timed landing-zone clearance; failure eliminates the squad. Main-quest completion is separate, and BO6 allows continued play afterward. [M4] [M5] [M6]

### Between matches: researched, equipped Augments

BO6 **does not simply reuse Cold War's cumulative skill tiers**. Players select a research target and earn in-game XP toward Augments for perks, Ammo Mods, and Field Upgrades. The launch design gives each item three Major and three Minor choices, but equips only **one Major and one Minor** at a time. **Synthesis:** This combines persistent unlocks with build selection, rather than automatically applying every researched effect simultaneously. [M5]
